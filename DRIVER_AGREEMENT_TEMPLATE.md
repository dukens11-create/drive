# FlupFlap Driver Agreement — DRAFT FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW

This is an operational drafting aid, not an executed contract.

Before a driver can accept live trips, final terms should cover:
- exact contracting entity and driver classification consistent with applicable law;
- driver eligibility, licensing, driving-record and background-check requirements;
- vehicle registration, inspection and insurance requirements;
- ongoing document expiration/reverification;
- platform access, dispatch acceptance, cancellations and prohibited conduct;
- fare calculation, FlupFlap service fee/commission, tips, Stripe Connect transfers and payout timing;
- tax responsibility and required tax reporting;
- safety, nondiscrimination, accessibility/service-animal obligations where applicable;
- privacy/location processing while online and during active trips;
- accident/incident reporting and cooperation;
- suspension/deactivation, appeal/review process where required;
- intellectual property/confidentiality;
- final dispute, governing-law and indemnity language approved for the launch jurisdiction.

The production onboarding UI must require the driver to affirm the final version and record the accepted version, timestamp, user ID and relevant electronic-consent evidence.

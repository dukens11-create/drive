Request FlupFlap account deletion

This page is for FlupFlap Rider, FlupFlap Driver and FlupFlap Eat account deletion requests.

Send the request from the email address associated with your account. Include only your account email/phone and the words “Account deletion.” Do not email card numbers, passwords, government ID images or banking credentials.

Email deletion request

What happens

FlupFlap will verify the request, disable access as appropriate, and delete or de-identify associated data that is not required to be retained for legitimate legal, tax, insurance, fraud-prevention, safety or accounting reasons. The final production retention policy must be approved before launch.

If you cannot access the account email, contact support@flupflap.com and support will provide a verification process.

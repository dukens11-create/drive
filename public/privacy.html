FlupFlap Privacy Policy

Pre-launch draft: this page must receive final legal/entity review before public launch.

FlupFlap processes account, trip/order, location, payment-provider, verification, device, safety and support information to provide ride-hailing and delivery services.

Location

Rider location is used for pickup, routes and trip progress. When a driver chooses to go online, FlupFlap Driver may collect location in the background, including when the app is closed or not in use, to keep the driver available for ride requests and to provide live trip progress. Background location is not collected solely for advertising.

Payments and verification

Payment and identity providers may process card, bank, identity, license or verification information. FlupFlap should retain only the data and provider identifiers needed to operate, reconcile and comply with applicable obligations.

Sharing

Information may be shared with the rider, driver, courier or merchant involved in a transaction and with service providers needed for payments, maps, cloud hosting, verification, communications, safety and support.

Deletion

You may request deletion through the FlupFlap account deletion page. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate legal, tax, insurance, safety, fraud-prevention or accounting requirements; the final policy must specify the applicable retention schedule.

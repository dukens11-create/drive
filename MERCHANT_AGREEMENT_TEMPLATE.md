# FlupFlap Merchant Agreement — DRAFT FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW

This is an operational drafting aid, not an executed contract.

Final merchant terms should cover:
- exact merchant and FlupFlap legal entities;
- authority to claim/control a restaurant listing;
- menu, price, tax, availability and image accuracy;
- food preparation, food safety, allergens and regulatory responsibility;
- permitted Square/Toast/Clover authorization and credential/token handling;
- order acceptance/rejection, refunds, chargebacks and customer remedies;
- commissions, fees, merchant settlement and payout timing;
- courier pickup procedures and order handoff;
- customer support and dispute cooperation;
- branding/content licenses;
- privacy/security obligations;
- suspension/termination;
- final liability, indemnity, dispute and governing-law provisions approved by counsel.

Merchant onboarding should record the agreement version and acceptance event.

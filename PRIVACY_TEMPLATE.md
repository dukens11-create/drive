FlupFlap provides ride-hailing, driver, food delivery, merchant, support, safety, and related marketplace services. This policy describes information handled when people use FlupFlap Rider, FlupFlap Driver, FlupFlap Eat, merchant tools, websites, and support services.

## Information we may collect
- Account information such as name, email, phone number, login and verification status.
- Rider and driver location information needed for pickup, routing, dispatch, trip progress and safety. A driver who chooses to go online may provide location in the background while the app is closed or not in use so dispatch and active-trip location remain available.
- Driver/merchant verification information, such as license, vehicle, insurance, business, identity-verification and background-check status. Sensitive verification documents should be handled by approved providers where possible.
- Trip/order information, including pickup/dropoff or delivery addresses, timestamps, route, fare/order amount, cancellations, ratings and support history.
- Payment and payout information. Payment card data should be tokenized/processed by the payment provider; FlupFlap may retain provider IDs, status, limited bank/account metadata and transaction records.
- Device, security, diagnostic and usage information used to operate, secure and improve the service.
- Messages, safety reports and customer-support information when users choose to contact FlupFlap or one another through supported features.

## How information is used
We use information to provide dispatch, navigation, trip/order fulfillment, payments, driver/merchant onboarding, fraud prevention, safety functions, support, service communications, legal/compliance operations and product reliability.

FlupFlap does not use driver background location solely for advertising.

## Sharing
Information may be shared as needed with the rider, driver, courier or merchant involved in a transaction; service providers such as payment, mapping, cloud, identity/background-check and communication providers; and authorities or other parties when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect safety, rights or the integrity of the service.

We do not sell payment card numbers, government identification documents or precise background location.

## Retention
Retention depends on the type of record and legitimate operational, safety, fraud-prevention, tax, accounting, insurance and legal obligations. Account deletion requests will delete or de-identify account data that FlupFlap is not required or permitted to retain. Any required retention must be documented in the production retention schedule.

## Security
FlupFlap uses access controls, encryption in transit, secret management, provider tokenization and audit/monitoring controls. No system can guarantee absolute security.

## User choices and rights
Users may update account information, change device permissions, go offline to stop driver dispatch/background-location use, and request account/data deletion at the public account-deletion page. Applicable privacy rights vary by location.

## Children
FlupFlap is not intended for children to independently create driver, merchant, or ride-service accounts where prohibited by applicable law.
